About Lifetime Immigration

Our story

Two Decades of Canadian Immigration

Lifetime Immigration helps families through the Canadian immigration process by following a defined, transparent method. We review your eligibility, advise you on the right pathway, prepare and file the application, prepare you for any interview, and liaise with the visa office until a decision is made.

Our objective is simple: find the immigration program that will actually let you qualify. That sometimes means telling an applicant that the route they had in mind is not the one that will work — or that now is not the right time. An honest answer at the start costs far less than a refusal later.

We work from two offices, in Etobicoke, Ontario and Port Louis, Mauritius, and serve clients in both English and French — including Quebec's separately administered programs.

Why it matters

Regulated, and Accountable For It

Only three groups may lawfully represent you for a fee in a Canadian immigration matter: lawyers, Quebec notaries, and consultants regulated by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants. Everyone else is operating outside the law, and you have no recourse when something goes wrong.

  • Listed on the CICC-CCIC public register, which anyone can search
  • Bound by a professional code of conduct and a complaints process
  • Required to carry professional liability insurance
  • Subject to mandatory continuing professional development
