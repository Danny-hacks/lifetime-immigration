Overview

How Express Entry Works

The Government of Canada chooses skilled workers based on their skills and their ability to contribute to Canada’s economy. Express Entry is the system that manages applications for three economic immigration programs.

To meet local labour market needs, candidates in the Express Entry pool can also be recruited by provinces and territories through the Provincial Nominee Program.

Federal Skilled Worker

Selection Factors

The Federal Skilled Worker Program targets skilled workers with foreign work experience who want to obtain permanent residence in Canada. If candidates meet all the minimum requirements, their applications are then assessed on the following factors.

  • Age
  • Education
  • Work experience
  • A valid job offer
  • English and/or French language skills
  • Adaptability — how well you are likely to settle here

The minimum requirements are a threshold, not a score. Skilled work experience, language ability and education must all be met before the selection factors are considered at all.

Work experience

Skilled Work Experience

Candidates must have worked in one of these National Occupational Classification job groups: managerial jobs (skill type 0), professional jobs (skill level A), or technical jobs and skilled trades (skill level B).

Candidates must show that, while working in their primary occupation, they performed the duties set out in the lead statement of the occupational description in the NOC — including all the essential duties and most of the main duties listed.

  • Within the last ten years
  • Paid work — volunteer work and unpaid internships do not count
  • In the same NOC as the job used for the immigration application
  • At least one year of continuous work, or 1,560 hours in total
The 1,560 hours

How the Hours Can Be Reached

One year of continuous full-time work is 30 hours a week for 12 months, which is 1,560 hours. That total can be reached in more than one way.

  • Full time at one job: 30 hours a week for 12 months
  • An equal amount part time: 15 hours a week for 24 months
  • Full time at more than one job: 30 hours a week for 12 months across several jobs

For part-time work, candidates can work more or fewer than 15 hours a week as long as it adds up to 1,560 hours.

Language

Language Ability

Applicants must take an approved language test in English or French evaluating writing, reading, listening and speaking, and reach the minimum level in all four abilities for their first official language: Canadian Language Benchmark 7 for English, or Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadienne 7 for French.

Applicants can also earn up to 30 additional points for strong French, even where French is their second language.

  • 15 additional points if you scored CLB 4 or lower in English, or took no English test
  • 30 additional points if you scored CLB 5 or higher across all four English abilities
Education

Educational Credentials

Applicants who went to school in Canada must have a certificate, diploma or degree from a Canadian secondary or post-secondary school. For a foreign education, candidates need a completed credential together with an Educational Credential Assessment report from an approved agency.

Language results are valid for two years from the test date, and must be valid on the day the permanent residence application is made. When candidates are invited to apply, they must check that their results will still be valid when they plan to apply — not merely when they entered the pool.

Questions

Express Entry Questions

Which programs does Express Entry manage?

Three: the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Federal Skilled Trades Program and the Canadian Experience Class.

Do I need a job offer?

A valid job offer is one of the selection factors assessed, but the minimum requirements are skilled work experience, language ability and education. Many candidates are invited without an offer.

Does part-time work count?

Yes. What matters is reaching 1,560 hours in your primary occupation. Fifteen hours a week for 24 months counts the same as 30 hours a week for 12 months.

What language level do I need?

A minimum of CLB 7 in English, or NCLC 7 in French, across all four abilities for your first official language.

How long are language results valid?

Two years from the date of the test result. They must be valid on the day you make your permanent residence application, so results taken early can expire at the wrong moment.

What if my education is from outside Canada?

You need the completed credential plus an Educational Credential Assessment report from an approved agency, confirming it is equivalent to the Canadian standard.
