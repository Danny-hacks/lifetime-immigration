Overview

Several Avenues, Not One

If you are a businessman, owner, entrepreneur or expansion manager looking to migrate to Canada, there may be several avenues to choose from.

Business immigration is designed to attract individuals with business or managerial expertise who can contribute to the growth and development of the Canadian economy. It also gives business owners and entrepreneurs the opportunity to invest in a Canadian business or acquire one.

  • Federal business immigration promotes economic development nationally
  • Several provincial business immigration programs run alongside it
  • Quebec operates its own investor, entrepreneur and self-employed programs

Start-Up Visa

Support From a Designated Organisation

The Start-Up Visa requires a letter of support from an organisation designated by the Government of Canada. There are three categories of designated organisation, and securing that support is the substantive part of the application.

  • Designated venture capital funds
  • Designated angel investor groups
  • Designated business incubators

The designated list changes. Organisations are added and removed over time, so the right target for your venture is confirmed at assessment rather than taken from a published list that may be out of date.

Quebec

Investor and Entrepreneur Programs

Quebec selects business immigrants against its own Economic Class selection grid. For the Investor Program, points are allocated across six factors: age, education level and area of training, management experience, language proficiency in English and French, family in Quebec, and the investment agreement. If you are not investing or starting a business, see Quebec Selection for the skilled worker route into the province.

Entrepreneurs can apply under one of two components: new businesses or start-ups established in the province with the support of a business incubator, accelerator or university; or owner-funded and operated businesses.

  • Applicants must be 18 or older
  • A business plan is an important factor in the entrepreneur assessment
  • French language proficiency is not a requirement for the entrepreneur program
Self-employed

Self-Employed Persons Program

Candidates with relevant experience in cultural activities or athletics are eligible for permanent residence under this program. Applicants must meet the minimum eligibility requirements and obtain the passing mark under the program’s selection criteria.

Eligible applicants must be able to show they have sufficient funds to finance their business and to settle in Canada with their dependants — a spouse or common-law partner and children.

  • Relevant experience in cultural activities or athletics
  • Must meet the minimum eligibility requirements and the passing mark
  • No regulated minimum net worth requirement for this program
Investment levels

Financial Requirements

The investor and entrepreneur programs set financial thresholds — a minimum investment, and in most cases a minimum net worth that must have been acquired legally. The entrepreneur categories additionally require a period of management experience and active participation in running the business.

Figures are confirmed at assessment, not quoted here. Investment and net worth thresholds for these programs are revised periodically, and a number published on a website is only accurate until the next revision. We give you the current figure for the route that suits you, in writing.

Questions

Business Immigration Questions

Who is business immigration for?

Business owners, entrepreneurs, investors and expansion managers with business or managerial expertise who can contribute to the growth of the Canadian economy.

What does the Start-Up Visa require?

Support from a designated organisation — a designated venture capital fund, a designated angel investor group, or a designated business incubator.

How does Quebec assess investors?

On a points grid covering six factors: age, education level and area of training, management experience, language proficiency in English and French, family in Quebec, and the investment agreement.

Do I need French for the Quebec entrepreneur program?

French language proficiency is not a requirement when applying to the entrepreneur program. A business plan, however, is an important factor.

Is there a minimum net worth for the self-employed program?

No. There is no regulated minimum net worth for the Self-Employed Persons Program, though you must show sufficient funds to finance your business and settle with your dependants.

How much do I need to invest?

It depends on the program, and the thresholds are revised periodically. Rather than publish a figure that may be superseded, we confirm the current requirement for your chosen route at the assessment stage.
