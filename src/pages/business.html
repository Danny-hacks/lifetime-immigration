Residence Through Enterprise

Business immigration is judged differently from skilled-worker immigration. Age and language still matter, but what carries the application is the strength and credibility of the venture, the source of the funds, and your track record of running something successfully.

These files take longer to prepare and involve more parties — designated organisations, provincial authorities, sometimes Quebec's own ministry. We tell applicants honestly at the assessment stage whether the business case is strong enough to be worth the investment.

Questions

Business Immigration Questions

How much do I need to invest?

It depends entirely on the programme. The federal Start-Up Visa has no fixed personal investment figure but requires backing from a designated organisation. Quebec and the provincial entrepreneur streams set their own investment and net worth thresholds, and those are revised periodically. We confirm the current figures for the route that suits you.

What is a designated organisation?

For the Start-Up Visa, a Canadian venture capital fund, angel investor group or business incubator approved to support applicants. Securing that support is the substantive hurdle in the programme — the immigration paperwork is the easier half.

Do I need a full business plan?

Yes, in practice. Business streams are judged on whether the venture is credible and whether you are capable of running it. A thin plan is the most common reason a strong applicant is refused.

Do I need to speak English or French?

Most business streams set a language requirement, and it is generally lower than the skilled-worker programs. It is rarely the deciding factor — the business case is.

Where does the money have to come from?

It must be legally obtained and you must be able to trace it. Source-of-funds documentation is often the longest part of a business file, and starting it early is the single best thing you can do to keep the application moving.

Is my business idea strong enough?

That is precisely what the assessment is for. We will tell you plainly if we do not think the case would survive scrutiny — before you spend anything on it.
