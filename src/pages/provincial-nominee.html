Provincial Nominee Program

How it works

Nominated by the Province That Needs You

The Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) is one of the fastest routes to permanent residence — alongside Express Entry, Quebec selection, family sponsorship and the business investor programs. We confirm which route fits your profile at assessment; this page covers the provincial nominee route in detail.

People who immigrate under the Provincial Nominee Program have the skill set, qualifications and work experience needed to make an economic contribution in the province or territory that nominates them, and are ready to establish themselves successfully as permanent residents.

To apply, you must be nominated by one of Canada’s nine participating provinces or territories. Each has an accord with the Government of Canada allowing it to propose immigrants who want to settle there — Quebec is the exception, and selects separately.

  • You apply to the province of your choice and complete its nomination process
  • The province considers your application against its immigration needs
  • It also assesses your bonafide purpose to settle in that province

Provincial nomination

Applying to a Province

If you are willing to migrate to Canada as a provincial nominee, you apply to the province of your choice and complete its provincial nomination process. The province considers your application on the basis of its immigration needs and your genuine purpose in settling there.

That second test matters. A nomination is granted on the understanding that you intend to live and work in that province, and the evidence for that intention forms part of the application.

Planning to settle in Quebec instead? Quebec is not part of the Provincial Nominee Program — it runs its own selection process. See Quebec Selection for how that route works.

Questions

Provincial Nominee Questions

How do I become a provincial nominee?

You apply to the province of your choice and complete its nomination process. The province considers the application against its own immigration needs and your genuine intention to settle there.

Is Quebec part of the Provincial Nominee Program?

No. Quebec runs its own selection under a separate agreement with the federal government and issues the Certificat de sélection du Québec before you apply federally for permanent residence. See Quebec Selection for that route.

Do I have to settle in the province that nominates me?

The nomination is granted on the basis that you intend to establish yourself there, and that intention is assessed as part of the application rather than assumed.

Can a province select me from Express Entry?

Yes. Provinces and territories can recruit candidates from the Express Entry pool to meet local labour market needs.
