After you land

Settlement Services

Settling in Canada looks straightforward until you are doing it. The challenges vary from family to family, and depend a great deal on how prepared you are before you arrive. Our settlement coordinators are based in Canada and work with newcomers to get established quickly.

  • The landing process at Pearson International Airport, and initial transportation
  • Temporary accommodation for the first few days, then help finding a rental — and furnishings if it is unfurnished
  • Applying for the basic cards: social insurance number, health card, public transport card
  • Opening a bank account, with a briefing on the products available
  • Registering with employment agencies, drafting a résumé and searching for work
  • Children's schooling, driving licences, property purchase and business set-up
Questions

Working With Us

Is the initial assessment really free?

Yes, and there is no obligation attached to it. We would rather spend an hour telling you that the timing is wrong than take on a file that was never going to succeed.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

A regulated consultant reads it and replies. If there is enough in your message to give a preliminary view, you will get one. If not, we will ask for the specific details we need — usually age, education, work history and language scores.

Are government fees included in your professional fees?

No, and no consultant can include them. Government processing fees, biometrics, medicals and language tests are paid separately and directly. We set out both sets of costs in writing before you commit, so there are no surprises later.

Can you represent me before IRCC?

Yes. As consultants regulated by the CICC-CCIC we can be named as your authorised representative, correspond with IRCC on your behalf and respond to requests on your file.

Do you help after the visa is issued?

Yes — that is the part most consultancies leave out. Our settlement coordinators in Canada cover the landing process, temporary accommodation, the basic cards, banking, schooling and job search.

What if my application is refused?

We analyse the reasons and tell you honestly whether a resubmission is realistic. Where IRCC issues a procedural fairness letter first, there is a window to respond before any decision is made — and what goes into that response matters a great deal.
