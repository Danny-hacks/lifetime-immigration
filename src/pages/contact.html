Questions

Before You Get in Touch

Is the first consultation free?

The eligibility assessment is free and carries no obligation. If your situation needs detailed legal analysis beyond that, we will tell you before any fee is discussed.

Can we meet in person?

Yes, at either office — Etobicoke in Ontario, or Port Louis in Mauritius. Please arrange an appointment first so a consultant is available for you.

Do you consult by video?

Yes. Most of our clients are outside Canada when we first speak to them, and files are handled remotely from start to finish.

What should I have ready?

Your age, highest completed education, work history with job titles and dates, any language test results, and details of family members who would come with you. If you have been refused before, bring the refusal letter.

Which languages do you work in?

English and French, including Quebec's separately administered programs.
