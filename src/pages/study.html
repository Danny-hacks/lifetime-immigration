Study in Canada

Study Now, Stay Later

A study permit is rarely the destination. For most of our clients it is the first step in a sequence: study at a designated learning institution, qualify for a post-graduation work permit, accumulate Canadian work experience, then apply for permanent residence through Express Entry or a provincial stream.

That sequence only works if the programme and institution are eligible at the outset. Choosing a course that does not carry post-graduation work permit eligibility is the single most expensive mistake we see.

Questions

Study Permit Questions

Will studying in Canada lead to permanent residence?

It can, and for many of our clients it is the most reliable route — but not automatically. The sequence is: study at an eligible institution, qualify for a post-graduation work permit, build Canadian work experience, then apply through Express Entry or a provincial stream. Each step has its own conditions.

Does every course qualify for a post-graduation work permit?

No, and this is the most expensive mistake we see. Eligibility depends on the institution, the programme and its length. Enrolling on a course that does not carry PGWP eligibility can cost you the entire pathway. We check this before you accept an offer.

Can I work while I am studying?

Study permit holders at designated learning institutions may generally work off campus without a separate work permit, subject to a weekly cap. That cap has changed more than once in recent years, so we confirm the rule that applies on the day you enrol.

What is a designated learning institution?

A school approved by a province or territory to host international students. Only DLIs can support a study permit, and only some DLI programmes carry post-graduation work permit eligibility — the two are not the same test.

Can my spouse and children come with me?

Usually. Accompanying family members apply alongside you, and depending on your programme and level of study your spouse may be eligible for an open work permit. The rules here have tightened, so this is worth confirming rather than assuming.

What is proof of funds?

Evidence that you can pay tuition and support yourself, and any family with you, without working. Officers look at where the money came from and how long it has been there, not only the balance on the day you apply.
