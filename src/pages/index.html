Questions

Common Questions

Do I actually need an immigration consultant?

No. You are entitled to prepare and submit your own application, and many people do. What a consultant adds is knowing which program you stand the best chance in, what evidence an officer will look for, and how to respond when something goes wrong. If your case is straightforward we will tell you so.

How do I check that a consultant is actually licensed?

Search the public register kept by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants. Only CICC-CCIC members, Canadian lawyers and Quebec notaries may lawfully charge a fee to represent you. Anyone else is operating outside the law, and you have no recourse if the file goes wrong.

What does the free assessment involve?

You give us your age, education, work history, language ability and family situation. We measure that against every program currently open and come back with the routes you qualify for — or an honest answer that none of them fit yet, and what would need to change.

Am I too old to immigrate to Canada?

Age is a scoring factor in the economic programs, not a cut-off. Points do decline as you get older, but applicants in their forties are approved regularly, usually because something else in the profile is strong — a provincial nomination, a job offer, Canadian experience or French.

Do I need a job offer?

Not for most permanent residence streams. A valid job offer adds points and can open provincial routes, but plenty of applicants are invited without one. Some programs — the Atlantic Immigration Program, for instance — do require an offer from a designated employer.

Can you help if I have already been refused?

Often, yes. The first step is understanding why. Some refusals are addressed and resubmitted; others point to an eligibility problem where a different program is the better answer. Bring the refusal letter to the assessment — it tells us far more than a summary does.

Do you work with clients outside Canada and Mauritius?

Yes. Our offices are in Etobicoke and Port Louis, but consultations run by video and files are handled remotely. We have clients across Africa, Asia and Europe.
